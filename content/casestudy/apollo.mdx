### The signal

just past $10M in revenue, with subscription to add and a Series B ahead.

### The challenge

turn a $10M hardware business into a recurring-revenue company and get it ready for its next raise, without a full executive bench.

### The Supernova advantage

operators who had solved this exact problem. Tonal's founder built subscription on top of hardware. The Beats executives scaled consumer hardware to a $3B exit.

### The impact

Apollo went from a hardware company to a subscription business.

Subscription attach rate rose from 20% to 90%, and subscription revenue grew 50% month over month for six months. Supernova's network also sourced growth capital quickly.

## They scaled to $10M alone. The next chapter needed people who had done it.

[Kathryn Fantauzzi](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kathryn-fantauzzi-b444ba1a) and [Dr. Dave Rabin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/david-rabin-md-phd-0a066110) took a wearable from a University of Pittsburgh lab to $10 million in revenue on their own. Then the work changed: adding subscription to hardware, and running finance through a raise.

Building an exec layer for the first time is a string of calls that look obvious and are expensive to get wrong.

Apollo needed people who had already made them.

Supernova brought in [Aly Orady](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aorady), founder and former CEO of [Tonal](https://www.tonal.com), who scaled it to unicorn status and built software before he built hardware. He advises [Apollo](https://apolloneuro.com) on corporate strategy. Supernova also placed Paul Eskenazi, former CFO of Beats by Dre, as corporate financial advisor, and TJ Grewal, former chief product officer at Beats by Dre, as an advisor. [Tim Chang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/timothy-chang-a06bb), a 2x Midas List VC, helped shape the Series B story. Supernova also referred and placed fractional executives and talent to grow the team, including an interim CMO, a COO, and other key positions.

The strategy came first. Apollo shifted to recurring revenue and began selling every wearable with its SmartVibes AI subscription at the point of sale, the way Tonal does. Attach rate went from 20% to 90%, and moving subscriptions out of the app stores cut fees from 30% to 5%.

Then Supernova got hands-on with growth. Working alongside Apollo's internal teams and partners, we went live with brand campaigns, DTC go-to-market, sales growth through influencer and affiliate channels, and conversion rate optimization.

On the capital side, Supernova's advisors rebuilt the investor deck and the financial story, coached the founders on pitching, and took reference calls from investors. They sourced funding from the network, and some invested directly.
