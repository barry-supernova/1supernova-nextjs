## He had exited three times. He wanted Superstars he trusted.

Dr. Leo Rastogi had built and exited three companies before SoulSpring, a physician-led platform for healthspan. He had a bridge round to close and a Series A to plan, and he needed a CMO and a pro to support the raise.

Anyone who has built companies before knows a title is easy to fill. Finding people who are great at the one thing you need, and who you would trust with your company, takes years.

Leo also wanted conscious capitalists, people who care about impact as much as profit.

So he tapped Supernova. In January 2022 Supernova deployed an exited founder as fractional Chief Marketing Officer, and a former $100M fund builder to help Leo raise. Both were Superstars he already trusted, each working in a zone of genius.

They got hands-on and got to work. They operationalized the strategy and executed it, across brand, go-to-market, and the Series A story.

The values matched too. Everyone at the table was building for impact and for profit.

Today SoulSpring serves 9,000+ clients a year across the U.S., Europe, and Mexico. It has partnered with WorldHotels Group to bring longevity clinics into luxury hotels worldwide, and is opening a clinic in St. Gallen, Switzerland.
