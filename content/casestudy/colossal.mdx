## Seed to decacorn in under four years.

[Ben Lamm](https://www.linkedin.com/in/benlamm) had built and exited companies before. He wanted to bring in the world's best to bring his mission to fruition.

Supernova started with [Colossal](https://colossal.com) at seed. It was our first portfolio company. Barry Stamos joined as founding advisor, recruited Peter Diamandis to the advisory board, and introduced investors who joined the first round.

Peter has since co-founded [NeoGenesis Capital](https://neogenesiscapital.com), a $200M venture fund built to back Colossal and the companies that grow out of its platform. That is what can happen when exited founders build together.
