## One Superstar for every executive.

[Roberto Salcedo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/roberto-salcedo-nieto) built [Baubap](https://baubap.com), a Y Combinator backed company, past 9 figures in ARR. At that size the founder can no longer coach every leader himself.

At that scale each executive is running a function bigger than any they have run before. An advisor for the CEO does not reach the CFO, the CTO, or the head of people.

Baubap came to Supernova to skill up its whole leadership team.

Supernova matched six exited founders and operators to six leaders, Roberto included, and his team chose the lineup. Each Superstar worked directly with one leader on that leader's priorities: fundraising, engineering, product, marketing, people, and risk. The full group came together in an all-hands Spark, and the team left with a fundraising plan and investor introductions.

Roberto sits at the center, with Andrew Bertolina as his own advisor. Every executive around him had a dedicated Supernova Superstar working on that function's priorities.
