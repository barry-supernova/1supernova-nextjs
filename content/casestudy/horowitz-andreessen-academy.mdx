## The round was nearly full. An exited founder opened the door.

The best seed rounds fill before most investors hear about them. If you are the founder, you know it from the other side: one slot left and ten funds asking for it.

Andreessen Horowitz incubated the [Horowitz Andreessen Academy](https://a16z.com/announcement/incubating-horowitz-andreessen-academy/), a San Francisco school for young builders, and led its $42 million round. Gagan Biyani, who co-founded Udemy and founded Maven, leads it as founder and CEO. The round also drew angels including Tobi Lütke of Shopify, Tony Xu of DoorDash, Garry Tan of Y Combinator, Adam D'Angelo of Quora, and Shyam Sankar of Palantir. Supernova participated through an allocation offered by an exited founder with Supernova who mentors Gagan.
